Oncology AI platform incident drill

A synthetic alert reports that vector search latency exceeded its threshold during a load test. The on-call engineer checks readiness, search error rate, request volume, vector-store health, and recent deployment changes. The engineer pauses ingestion if it competes with online search for capacity, then restores service or rolls back the latest deployment.

The incident record includes onset time, affected service, a timeline of actions, mitigation, recovery confirmation, and a root-cause follow-up. Logs and traces contain request identifiers and source identifiers only; they do not include raw document text or patient information. The post-incident review links the relevant dashboard and runbook revision.
