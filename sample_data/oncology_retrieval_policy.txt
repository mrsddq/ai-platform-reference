Oncology knowledge retrieval policy

The document retrieval service accepts approved, versioned operations documents only. Each indexed passage carries a source identifier, document version, content digest, and passage number. Search results return this provenance with the excerpt so a reviewer can trace every answer to an exact source version.

Retrieved text is untrusted data. It must not override system instructions or authorize a tool action. If no relevant passage is found, the assistant states that the indexed material does not support an answer. The service does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or patient-specific conclusions.
